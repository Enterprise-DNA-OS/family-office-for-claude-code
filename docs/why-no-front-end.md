# Why there is no front end

A family office platform holds a short list of things: the people, the trusts and companies they own or benefit from, what each one holds, what it was last worth, what has been promised to funds, and the dates and papers the law expects. Asora charges for the screens on top of those records and for the feeds that fill them.

The screens were the product because the records were hard to ask about. They are not any more. Open this folder in Claude Code and ask "how much can the funds still call, and which entity pays?" or "what does James own outright, against what sits in the trust?". It runs the query and answers in plain words.

## What you gain

- **Answers to your own questions.** A dashboard shows what the vendor charted. Here the ten questions in the README are a start, and `/new-view` adds the eleventh.
- **No tier set by your net worth.** Asora prices by the wealth you track. A database does not care how much is in it.
- **The family's records stay with the family.** One database, on a server you choose. Back it up, read it from anything, leave any time.
- **The legal side in the same place.** Trust papers, beneficiary notices, Division 7A repayments and filing dates sit next to the money, so one command finds what is breached.

## What a screen gives that this does not

- **Live bank and custodian feeds.** Asora pulls statements in automatically. Here values arrive by import (the custodian's or Asora's export) or by telling Claude Code the number. Enterprise DNA connects the feeds a family needs in the custom version.
- **A phone app for the family.** Family members who want to look on a phone get nothing from this folder. A read-only page or a printed quarterly report covers most of it, and both are part of what we build.
- **Drag and drop.** Ownership charts are a table you ask about, not boxes you move.

## Who this fits

A family office of one to five people, or an accounting or advisory firm running the office for a family, who would rather ask than click. If the family wants an app on every phone and live feeds from day one, keep Asora, or have us build those on top of this.
