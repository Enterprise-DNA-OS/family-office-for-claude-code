---
description: Who benefits from each trust, and whether basic trust information was given (Trusts Act 2019 s51).
---

1. Run `npm run family -- beneficiaries [TRUST] --json`.
2. For anyone with no date and no reason, say so. Record what happened with `npm run family -- inform TRUST --member=NAME --on=DATE`, or the trustees' reason for not informing them with `--reason=TEXT` (s53 lists the factors).
3. To prepare the information, run `/draft-beneficiary-letter`. The trustees or their lawyer decide; this records their decision.
